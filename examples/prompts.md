# Prompts to paste into Claude

With the Bookipi skill or connector installed, these run whole workflows from
one message. Claude does the lookups itself and stops to ask before it sends,
charges or records anything.

## Money in

> Who owes me money? Group it by how late it is, and draft a reminder for
> anything more than 30 days overdue. Show me the drafts before sending.

> I got paid by bank transfer. Here's my bank export — match the deposits to my
> invoices and mark the matching ones paid.

> Create an invoice for Acme Corp: 10 hours of consulting at $150, due in 14
> days. Show me a preview first.

> Make a payment link for $250 for the Wayne workshop deposit and email it to
> bruce@wayne.example.

## Winning work

> Draft a proposal for Acme: a website redesign, three pages plus a blog, about
> $6,000 over six weeks. Send it to Maria once I've approved it.

> Acme accepted the proposal. Draft the eSign contract from it and send it to
> Maria for signature.

> Has Bruce signed the MSA yet? If he has, turn the proposal into an invoice.

> Which deals have gone quiet? Draft a short follow-up for each one that's been
> stuck in proposal for more than two weeks.

## Knowing where you stand

> Give me my morning brief.

> Show revenue for this quarter, my top five customers, and anything unusual I
> should look at.

> Show me everything about Wayne Enterprises: invoices, payments, deals,
> emails and meetings.

> What was agreed in yesterday's meeting with Acme? List the action items.

## Receipts

> (Attach photos or PDFs of receipts.) Log these as expenses.

## Make it recurring

> Every Monday at 8am, send me a digest of last week: what came in, what's
> overdue, and which deals need attention.
